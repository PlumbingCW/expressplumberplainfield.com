Emergency services and planned work

Some services are urgent by nature. Burst pipes, sewage backups, leaking water heaters, failed sump pumps and boilers that stop in winter cannot wait for a convenient day. Those pages explain what to do in the first few minutes, and our dispatch line is open 24/7 for them. Same-day visits are booked when a plumber is available.

Other services are planned. A tankless upgrade, a repipe, a new bathroom or a camera look at an old sewer line is better done on your schedule, with the permit and the parts settled in advance. Planning also tends to cost less than the same job done in a rush.

How we handle every job

Every visit begins with a diagnosis. We find the cause, explain it in plain terms and give you the price before work starts. If the cheapest fix is a small repair, we say so. If a larger job is really needed, we show you why. In older Plainfield houses, where pipes of different ages are joined together, that honesty matters more than anywhere.

Many jobs touch the City's permit rules. The City of Plainfield's homeowner manual lists installing a hot water heater and rearranging plumbing pipes as permit work, and it says ordinary maintenance does not need one. The Construction Division at 908-226-2665 can tell you where your project falls.
