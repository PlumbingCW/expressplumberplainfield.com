Privacy Policy

How Plainfield Express Plumbing handles calls, recordings and website data.

Effective November 13, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Plainfield Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call (908) 357-1542. This website has no forms. The main way you contact us is by phone.

Calls and call recording

Calls to our dispatch line may be recorded and monitored for quality, training and dispute resolution. By continuing a call after hearing or seeing this notice, you consent to the recording. We may keep the phone number you call from, the time and length of the call, and the details you give us about your plumbing problem, so we can send the right plumber.

Information you give us

When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem. We use it to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, schedule work and follow up. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share job details with the plumbers and service providers who do the work, and with companies that help us run our phone and scheduling systems. We may disclose information when the law requires it or to protect safety.

Website data

Our web host and any analytics tools we use may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We use it to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. Your browser lets you block or delete cookies.

Third-party content

Pages on this site embed a Google map and load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may collect data under its own privacy policy when those load.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by calling (908) 357-1542 or emailing contact@expressplumberplainfield.com. New Jersey residents may have additional rights under state law, and we will honor valid requests.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
