When to call right away in North Plainfield

Dispatch covers North Plainfield around the clock. Same-day visits are booked when a technician is free. A few situations cannot wait for morning.

  • Water rising in a basement from the floor drain, a sump pit that has stopped or a failed pump.
  • Sewage or gray water backing up in the lowest drain in the house.
  • A burst or split pipe, with water running until the main is closed.
  • No hot water with a leaking tank, or any gas smell near a heater or boiler.

What North Plainfield homes call us about most

North Plainfield is a small borough of about 2.8 square miles with 8,055 housing units at the 2020 census. That works out to roughly 2,850 homes per square mile, so lots are tight, basements are common and neighboring houses often share a similar age and layout.

That density shows up in the calls. Basement water, sump pump failures and main line backups come first. Sump pump installation and repair and drain cleaning lead the list, followed by sewer line cleaning and water heater repair.

Hurricane Ida and the basements it filled

On September 2, 2021, the borough issued a press release after Hurricane Ida's remnants hit. It reported floodwater on Somerset Street strong enough to move the center barriers on Route 22, flooding that spread onto Mountain Avenue and toward Greenbrook Road, and two homes pushed off their foundations. More than 70 disabled vehicles were removed from roads and lawns.

The release also said the fire department kept a list of residents who needed basement pump-outs, and told residents with utility damage to hire a licensed plumber or electrician. For a homeowner, the lesson is practical. A sump pump with a battery backup and a clear floor drain are worth having before a storm, because crews are stretched thin during one.

Washington Park and the older blocks

The Washington Park Historic District was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1987, and the borough has many older houses besides. Homes from earlier decades often have cast iron or clay drain lines, galvanized supply pipe and a boiler or a tank heater that has been replaced more than once.

On those blocks, a slow drain is often a pipe-condition issue. We recommend a sewer camera inspection when a main line clogs more than once, so the repair matches the real problem.

Water, sewer and permits in the borough

New Jersey American Water lists North Plainfield Borough in its service territory, so water bill and meter questions go to the utility. The borough is also one of the eight municipalities served by the Plainfield Area Regional Sewerage Authority, and its municipal website hosts the authority's page.

North Plainfield is in Somerset County, not Union County, so permits and inspections come from the borough and not from Plainfield. The borough offices can be reached at 908-769-2900. Ask them what your project needs before work starts.
