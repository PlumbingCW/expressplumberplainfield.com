Putnam-Watchung Historic District, Plainfield.

Plumber in Putnam-Watchung, Plainfield, NJ

The Putnam-Watchung Historic District is a residential area centered on Putnam and Watchung Avenues, close to downtown Plainfield. Call (908) 357-1542, describe the problem, and we will explain what service is available.

Call (908) 357-1542

What the district is, and what is not documented

The City of Plainfield lists the Putnam-Watchung Historic District among its ten districts and publishes a map of it. The city's list shows a local designation in 1987 and no National Register listing.

We did not find a written boundary for the district. Aggregator street data for the area lists Watchung Avenue and Putnam Avenue in the 1000 blocks, plus Richmond Street, Carlton Avenue, Kensington Avenue, Webster Place, Hillside Avenue and Colonial Circle. That is a guide, not an official definition.

If you are not sure whether your street is inside, check the city's district map.

Older homes close to downtown

A local real estate guide describes the district's houses as historic homes from the early 1900s, often with three to seven bedrooms, within reach of the Plainfield rail station and downtown.

The City-Data aggregator counts about 299 housing units in its version of the area, and 192 of them are dated 1939 or earlier. The site draws its boundary from mapping data, so we use the figure only as a rough indicator of age.

Age alone does not tell us what is inside a wall. We make no claim about pipe materials here.

A practical way to approach a plumbing problem in an older house

  1. Find the shutoff. Locate the main water valve and every fixture valve before you need them. Label them.
  2. Note when it happens. A noise that comes only when a certain fixture runs points to that line. A stain that grows after rain may have another source.
  3. Photograph it. Pictures of the stain, the pipe or the fixture help when you describe the problem on the phone.
  4. Ask about the fix and the price. Ask what will be done, what it will cost and what could change that.

For a stain with no visible source, see leak detection. For drains that run slowly in several fixtures, see drain cleaning. For a water heater that fails in a large house, see water heater replacement.

What to do before help arrives

  • Shut off the water to the affected fixture, or the main if needed.
  • Keep power off in any area where water is near outlets or a panel.
  • Move valuables up and photograph damage for your insurer.
  • Write down what you saw before you call.

Historic district note

Changes visible from a public right of way need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Plainfield Historic Preservation Commission. Plumbing is mostly interior, but if any part of the job touches the outside of the house, ask the Commission at 908-753-3421 first.

What affects the price of a repair in an older house

  • How far the water travelled.A stain far from the source can mean opening more of a wall or ceiling to trace it.
  • Fixture age.An old fixture may have no parts available, which can turn a repair into a replacement.
  • Access.Finished walls, tile and plaster add work to reach a pipe.
  • Scope.A drain clearing, a water heater swap and a repipe are very different jobs.

Ask what will be done, what it will cost, and what could change that. Call (908) 357-1542 to describe the job first.
