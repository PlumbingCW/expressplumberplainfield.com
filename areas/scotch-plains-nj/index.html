A township of 25,000 people on a ridge

Scotch Plains had 24,968 residents at the 2020 census and covers about 9.06 square miles on a ridge in Union County. It was incorporated as Fanwood Township in 1878 and took its present name in 1917. ZIP code 07076 covers the township.

A township this size has a wide mix. Older neighborhoods near Front Street and the Raritan Valley Line sit alongside larger lots toward the Watchung Reservation. The plumbing differs just as much from one street to the next.

Robinson's Branch, the Reservation and tree roots

Robinson's Branch, a branch of the Rahway River, runs through the township to the Robinson's Branch Reservoir, and the 1,945-acre Watchung Reservation covers the north side. Many streets here are lined with large, long-established trees.

Large trees and old lateral pipes are a bad pairing. Roots find the joints in clay pipe and the cracks in cast iron, and the same line then clogs every spring. For these calls we start with a camera look, then choose between sewer line cleaning, hydro jetting and sewer line repair.

Historic houses and aging systems

The township includes some very old buildings. The Stage House Inn dates to 1737, the Osborn-Cannonball House Museum stands at 1840 Front Street, and the Aunt Betty Frazee House at 1451 Raritan Road is a colonial-era farmhouse on the state and national historic registers. Most homes are far newer, but plenty were built long before modern plumbing codes.

Older houses often combine original pipe with piecemeal updates, and a basement boiler or a heater that was installed decades ago. When one of those systems fails, we explain whether a repair holds or whether replacement or boiler repair is the better call.

Water, sewer and storm recovery

New Jersey American Water lists Scotch Plains Township in its service territory, and the township is one of the eight municipalities served by the Plainfield Area Regional Sewerage Authority. Storm damage is a township concern too. After Hurricane Ida, the township's September 7, 2021 notice asked residents to report damage at nj.gov/ida and to keep photos and receipts for assistance and insurance.

If a plumbing failure causes water damage, take the same steps. Photograph everything before cleanup and keep every receipt.

Getting around the township

Route 28 runs briefly through the center of town and U.S. Route 22 crosses the north. Raritan Road, Rahway Road and Front Street are the main local routes, and the Raritan Valley Line bisects the township with the nearest passenger station in Fanwood. We plan dispatch around those routes.

Safety steps if water enters a Scotch Plains basement

  • Keep out of standing water if it could touch outlets, appliances or the panel.
  • Turn off power at the main breaker only if you can reach it from dry ground.
  • Call us for the pump, drain or line problem and call your insurer for the claim.
  • Photograph the damage before moving items so your claim has a record.
