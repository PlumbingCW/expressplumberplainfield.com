Van Wyck Brooks Historic District, Plainfield.

Plumber in Van Wyck Brooks, Plainfield, NJ

Van Wyck Brooks is the largest of Plainfield's residential historic districts, with big houses built mostly between 1875 and 1925. Bigger houses mean more fixtures and longer pipe runs. Call (908) 357-1542 and tell us what is happening.

A neighborhood named for a writer

The district is named for the author and critic Van Wyck Brooks, who grew up on West Eighth Street and won the 1937 Pulitzer Prize. The district's own association calls it the largest of Plainfield's six residential districts, with 152 properties.

The National Register summary lists bounding streets including Plainfield Avenue, West Eighth Street, Park Avenue, West Ninth Street, Madison Avenue and Randolph Road.

The city says This Old House magazine named it one of the best places in the country to buy an old house.

How the houses came to be

The district's association explains that rail commuters to New York began settling here. Most of the large houses date from 1875 to 1925.

The district's houses sit on land from a farm that was subdivided in the 1850s. The Plainfield Public Library is the only public building in it.

What size means for plumbing

Large houses generally have more bathrooms, more fixtures and longer runs of supply and drain pipe than a small house. This is general plumbing information, and we are not claiming anything about the pipes in a particular Van Wyck Brooks home.

What to do if water is spraying

  • Close the main water valve, usually near where the line enters the house or by the meter.
  • Turn off power to a wet area only if you can reach the switch without stepping in water.
  • Open a low faucet to drain the lines once the main is off.
  • Take photos before cleanup, and call a plumber and your insurer.

Rules for historic houses

Owners in a historic district apply to the Historic Preservation Commission for a Certificate of Appropriateness when changes are visible from a public right of way. The page treats this as exterior work.

Urgent repairs to prevent imminent damage can go to an emergency hearing. Contact the Commission at 908-753-3421 before planning any exterior element of a plumbing job.

What affects the price of work in a large house

  • Size and floors.More floors and longer pipe runs mean more time to reach, test and repair a line.
  • Fixture count.A repair that touches several bathrooms is a different job from one fixture.
  • Access.A finished basement, plaster walls or tile around a pipe add work to reach it.
  • Exterior elements.A vent or line that shows outside may add an application to the Commission.
  • Equipment.A large house may need a larger tank or more than one heater, which changes the cost of replacement.
