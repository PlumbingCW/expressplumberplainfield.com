Hillside Avenue Historic District, Plainfield.

Plumber on Hillside Avenue, Plainfield, NJ

One avenue, a clear boundary

Unlike some districts, this one has a simple definition. It runs along Hillside Avenue from Watchung Avenue to Martine Avenue. The city's list shows a local designation in February 1981, and the National Register listing followed in June 1982.

The district has 33 contributing buildings. The city describes the Hillside, Crescent, Van Wyck Brooks and Netherwood Heights districts as notable, active historic districts with house tours and a sense of community.

Colonial Revival houses of the early 1900s

Most houses on the avenue are Colonial Revival and were built in the first quarter of the 20th century. A few earlier houses are Queen Anne or Second Empire in style.

One landmark is 999 Hillside Avenue, a Colonial Revival replica of the Longfellow House in Cambridge, Massachusetts. It was built in 1907 by architect Alexander Milne for William A. Conner.

We cannot say what pipes, fixtures or heating systems these houses have. That depends on each owner's renovations.

Questions worth asking before work on a large older house

  • How old is the water heater, and where does its relief valve discharge?
  • Is there a floor drain, and has anyone checked where it leads?
  • Are there any shutoff valves that have never been turned in years?
  • Which rooms have been renovated, and which still have original fixtures?

If a tank fails, see water heater replacement. For a stain on a ceiling, see leak detection. For a drain that backs up, see drain cleaning.

What to do if water is spraying

  • Close the main water valve first.
  • Open a low faucet to drain the lines.
  • Stay away from water near outlets or an electrical panel.
  • Photograph damage and call a plumber and your insurer.

Historic district rules

The Plainfield Historic Preservation Commission reviews changes visible from a public right of way. A plumbing job that adds a vent, a meter box or any exterior element should be discussed with the Commission at 908-753-3421 before it starts.

What affects the price of a repair in a large older house

  • Equipment.The size of the water heater, and where it sits, changes the cost of repair or replacement.
  • Access.Finished rooms, tile or plaster around a pipe add work to reach it.
  • Fixture age.An older fixture may need an adapter or a replacement instead of a repair.
  • Exterior elements.A vent or line that shows from the street may add an application to the Commission.

Ask what the work involves and what it will cost before it starts. Call (908) 357-1542 to talk through the problem first.
