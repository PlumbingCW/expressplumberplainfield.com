Service Areas Near Plainfield, NJ

We dispatch from Plainfield to the nearby towns of North Plainfield, Scotch Plains, South Plainfield, Piscataway and Fanwood.

Plainfield and the towns around it

We are based at 168 East Front Street in Plainfield and dispatch across the surrounding towns. Each town has its own page, because the plumbing problems differ from one place to the next. North Plainfield has a flood history. Scotch Plains has big trees and old laterals. South Plainfield mixes homes and businesses. Piscataway is large and full of rental housing. Fanwood is a compact borough built around a historic rail district.

North Plainfield, Somerset County

Dense, older blocks on the Somerset County side of the line, with a flood history that shapes the work.

ZIP: 07060, 07063

Scotch Plains, Union County

A large, wooded township on a ridge, with older homes, mature lots and root-prone sewer laterals.

ZIP: 07076

South Plainfield, Middlesex County

A Middlesex County borough with a strong commercial base, Cedar Brook Park and a highway-linked grid.

ZIP: 07080

Piscataway, Middlesex County

A very large township with many sections, Rutgers campuses and a big rental and multi-unit base.

ZIP: 08854, 08855

Fanwood, Union County

A compact Union County borough built around its 1890s rail station and a National Register district.

ZIP: 07023

Plainfield ZIP codes

The city's street addresses use 07060, 07062 and 07063. The 07061 code is a post office box ZIP, tied to the post office on Watchung Avenue, and street delivery in that area uses 07060. If you give us your address, we do not need the ZIP code to find you.

Within the city we work in the downtown blocks along East Front Street and Park Avenue, and in neighborhoods including the Crescent Area, Van Wyck Brooks, Netherwood Heights and the Civic Historic District. Cedar Brook Park lies along the brook that the city's floodwater drains into.

How we reach you

Dispatch comes from Plainfield, so the neighboring boroughs are a short drive. Local roads such as Park Avenue, Front Street, Route 22 and Stelton Road matter more than the map distance, and we plan around them. We do not promise an arrival window, because traffic, weather and the emergencies ahead of you change it. We tell you what to expect when you call.

Different towns have different permit offices and different water and sewer arrangements. New Jersey American Water lists Plainfield, North Plainfield, Scotch Plains and Fanwood in its service territory, and the Plainfield Area Regional Sewerage Authority serves eight municipalities including most of the towns on this page.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. We cover more ground than these five pages describe. See our full list of services for what we can do once we arrive.
