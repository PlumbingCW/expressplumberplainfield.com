A borough split off from Piscataway in 1926

South Plainfield was carved out of Piscataway and incorporated in 1926. At the 2020 census it had 24,338 residents, its highest count ever, and 8,432 housing units. ZIP code 07080 covers the borough, which touches Plainfield and Scotch Plains to the north.

Its local place names include Avon Park, Holly Park, Samptown and Hadley Airport. Interstate 287 runs through the south end of the borough, with exits 4 and 5, and county roads Park Avenue, Stelton Road and New Durham Road mark its edges.

A borough with businesses as well as homes

South Plainfield is home to companies such as Tumi, PTC Therapeutics and a Lincoln Tech campus, along with the Plainfield Curling Club. Shops, workshops and small industrial users line the major roads, and many of them depend on plumbing that must work every day.

We handle commercial plumbing for these businesses, including floor drains, water heaters and fixture repairs, and we can schedule work outside business hours. Residential calls here lean toward drain cleaning, water heater repair and toilet repair.

Cedar Brook Park and what Ida did to the borough

Cedar Brook Park spans South Plainfield and Plainfield. Brooks and low parkland look peaceful until a storm arrives, and Hurricane Ida showed that in 2021. News 12 reported on September 15, 2021 that a South Plainfield family's home flooded with up to nine feet of water, and that their flood insurer would not cover the repair costs.

That story points to a general rule. Water that enters through the ground is usually treated differently by insurers than water from a burst pipe, and policies vary. Read your own coverage before a storm, and document damage right away. For the plumbing side, a sump pump with a battery backup and a clear floor drain are the first line of defense.

Water and sewer service

New Jersey American Water lists South Plainfield Borough in its Raritan system, in part, so confirm with the utility if you are unsure which water system serves your address. The borough is also one of the eight municipalities served by the Plainfield Area Regional Sewerage Authority.

A lateral that runs from the house to the street is generally the homeowner's responsibility. When a main line backs up repeatedly, a sewer camera inspection shows the cause.

Winter freeze and shop plumbing

Garages, workshops and unheated storage rooms in South Plainfield's commercial areas are where pipes freeze first. A supply line to a utility sink in an unheated bay can split overnight. If you run a business here, know where the shutoff for each building zone is, and mark it clearly. Our burst pipe repair page explains the first steps.

Residential streets and older housing

Away from the highways, South Plainfield has quiet residential streets with a mix of post-war houses and newer builds. Common calls include water heaters at the end of their life, kitchen drains with years of grease and toilets that run. A water heater repair or faucet repair is often a quick visit.
