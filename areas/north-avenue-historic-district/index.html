North Avenue Historic District, Plainfield (commercial).

Plumber in the North Avenue Historic District, Plainfield, NJ

This is Plainfield's commercial historic district, a row of Victorian brick buildings built around the train stations. Many house a shop below and apartments above. Call (908) 357-1542 and describe the building and the problem.

Call (908) 357-1542

A commercial district, not a residential one

The North Avenue Historic District is a commercial district. Its National Register nomination describes masonry Victorian commercial buildings that grew up around the Central Railroad of New Jersey stations. We do not present it as a residential subdivision.

The district runs along North Avenue from Park Avenue to Watchung Avenue and covers about nine acres. It has 33 properties, and most date from 1875 to 1930.

The city lists a local designation and a National Register listing in the 1980s.

Shops below, apartments above

The nomination says the buildings were built for shops, hotels, offices and services, and that some upper floors were used as living quarters. It says some buildings still contain apartments today.

That mix shapes plumbing calls in a general way. A problem in a shop may involve the tenant, the landlord, and residents upstairs. We cannot say what any one building's plumbing looks like.

Services for older mixed-use buildings

See commercial plumbing for the general scope. A camera inspection can show the condition of a drain line without digging. Drain cleaning clears a stoppage, and leak detection helps trace a stain through several floors.

What to do before help arrives: building leak or backup

  • Close the nearest shutoff valve or the building main.
  • Warn occupants on floors below the leak and keep customers away from wet floors.
  • Keep power off near water, and cover or move stock and equipment where safe.
  • Photograph the damage and tell the property owner and the insurer.

Historic district note

The Historic Preservation Commission reviews changes visible from a public right of way. For a commercial building, that can include signs, vents and other exterior elements. Contact the Commission at 908-753-3421 before planning any exterior element of a plumbing job.

What affects the price of a repair in a mixed-use building

  • Business hours.Work that must happen outside business hours can change how a job is scheduled and priced.
  • Shared lines.A drain or supply line that serves a shop and apartments above is a bigger repair.
  • Scope.Clearing a stoppage costs less than repairing a damaged drain line a camera has found.
  • Records.Owners, tenants and insurers may each need a written record of the repair.

Ask what the work involves and what it will cost before it starts. Call (908) 357-1542 to describe the building and the problem first.

What we could not confirm
