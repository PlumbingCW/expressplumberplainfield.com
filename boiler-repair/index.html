When a boiler problem is an emergency

A boiler failure in January affects pipes, comfort and safety. Gas smells, carbon monoxide alarms and water leaking from the boiler are urgent. We book same-day visits for no-heat calls when a technician is free.

  • No heat and the boiler is off, locked out or showing an error.
  • Water leaking from the boiler, a valve or the pipes around it.
  • A gas smell, a yellow flame or a carbon monoxide alarm sounding.
  • Banging, kettle-like boiling noises or a pressure gauge in the red.

Safe steps before we arrive

  1. Gas smell means leave. Get everyone out, do not flip switches, and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  2. Carbon monoxide alarm means leave. Go outside, call 911 and do not return until the building is cleared.
  3. Check the basics. Make sure the thermostat is calling for heat, the boiler switch is on and the fuel supply is open.
  4. Do not bleed or drain a hot system. Hot water can scald. Leave pressure and drain valves alone.
  5. Protect pipes in the cold. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls and keep a trickle at a vulnerable tap if the house is losing heat.

A boiler that needs repeated repairs and is decades old may be cheaper to replace. We explain the repair-or-replace numbers plainly.

Plainfield note: old houses and hot water heat

Plainfield's older neighborhoods, from the Crescent Area to Van Wyck Brooks, are full of houses built between the 1870s and the early 1900s. Many were heated by steam or hot water boilers originally, and some still are. A boiler can outlast several owners, but its radiators, piping and controls age too.

Old systems often have mixed piping, undersized circulators or controls from different decades. We read the system as a whole and tell you whether a part replacement will hold or the system needs a bigger decision.

Maintenance that prevents no-heat calls

  • Have the boiler serviced once a year before heating season.
  • Check the pressure gauge monthly. Most residential systems run in a stable range shown on the unit.
  • Bleed trapped air from radiators when they stay cold at the top.
  • Keep the area around the boiler clear and the combustion air supply open.
  • Test carbon monoxide alarms each month.

If your boiler also makes your domestic hot water, a failure can leave you without both. See water heater repair if the tank is a separate unit.

What affects the price of boiler repair

  • Part and system type.A thermostat costs less than a circulator pump or a gas valve.
  • Fuel type.Gas and oil systems differ in parts and service.
  • Age and parts availability.Old boilers may need hard-to-find parts.
  • Access.Tight basements take longer.
  • Time of day.After-hours dispatch is priced differently from a scheduled visit.

Carbon monoxide and boilers

  • Fit a working carbon monoxide alarm near the bedrooms and on each floor with a fuel-burning appliance.
  • If an alarm sounds, get everyone outside into fresh air and call 911. Do not go back in to look for the cause.
  • Headache, dizziness or nausea that eases when you leave the house can be a warning sign. Tell the responders.
  • Never run a boiler with a cracked flue or a scorched, sooty burner area. Turn it off and call us.
