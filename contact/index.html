What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What the water is doing, such as leaking, backing up or not heating.
  • Whether you have closed the main water valve.
  • Whether anyone is at risk, for example from a gas smell or standing water near electricity.
  • The brand and age of a water heater or boiler, if you know it.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Hours

Dispatch is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Scheduled work, such as a planned water heater installation or a camera inspection, is set by phone at a time that suits you. Browse our plumbing services if you are not sure what you need.
